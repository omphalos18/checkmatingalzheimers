Sponsors & Partners

The people and businesses behind us.

Checkmating Alzheimer's runs on the support of chess clubs, universities, and local businesses who believe in what we're doing. Here's who's helped make it possible, and how you can join them.

Current Sponsors & Partners

Grateful for their support.

Alzheimer's Association

Our primary charity partner. Tournament entry fees and donations go directly to their South Carolina chapter.

Clemson University Chess Club

Partners with us on tournaments and on our independent research collaboration with the Cognition and Decision Science Lab.

Furman University Chess Club

Co-hosts community tournaments and helps grow chess across the Upstate.

SC Chess Association

Supports our USCF-rated tournaments and connects us with chess clubs across the state.

Columbia Chess Club

Hosts and co-organizes tournaments across the Midlands region.

Greenville Chess Club

Our home-base club in the Upstate, supporting weekly play and tournaments.

AC

Anderson Chess Club

One of our longest-running weekly chess sessions for senior residents and student volunteers.

Sponsorship Levels

How to become a sponsor.

By sponsoring our initiatives, you support the development of chess statewide and Alzheimer's care and research through the Alzheimer's Association. We're working toward raising $10,000 by the end of 2026, every level of sponsorship moves us closer.

Supporter$50 Advocate$250 Pioneer$500+
Social media recognition on our Instagram
Website feature (size & placement varies by tier)
Company featured at #CheckAlz events
Online meeting with project leaders (Q&A)
Logo on the Checkmating Alzheimer's t-shirt
Alzheimer's Association certificate & customized plaque
Optional: speak at the introduction of a tournament

Sponsoring at any level aligns you with a cause that's moving chess, and Alzheimer's awareness, forward across South Carolina.

Ready to sponsor or donate?

Online donations and sponsorships are accepted through the Alzheimer's Association's Do What You Love platform for Checkmating Alzheimer's. Company matches are encouraged, just list your employer when you give.
