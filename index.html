Our Mission

A personal story, turned into a movement anyone can join.

After seeing how Alzheimer's affected his own family, co-founder Caton Tsao and fellow chess player Allen Hong turned to something they already knew well, chess, as a way to support cognitive health and bring people together.

What began as two students hosting local fundraising tournaments has grown into a multi-city initiative reaching more players and more communities every season.

What We Do

How chess fights back.

From community fundraising to senior outreach, every program moves the mission forward.

01

Tournaments for a Cause

USCF-rated tournaments across South Carolina where every entry fee goes to the Alzheimer's Association's #TheLongestDay campaign. All skill levels welcome.

Explore tournaments →
02

Chess for Seniors

Free weekly chess classes at retirement communities, bringing cognitive stimulation and real social connection to the people most at risk.

See senior outreach →

Outside of our community programs, co-founders Caton Tsao and Aman Sreshta are collaborating with Dr. Kaileigh Byrne and the Clemson Cognition and Decision Science Lab. Their independent student research explores chess as a mentally stimulating activity, and whether regular engagement can help support cognitive function in older adults. Learn more →
